IATA New Distribution Capability (NDC) — AirShopping Architecture Guide

Standard Version: 21.3 | Category: Supplier & API Architecture

1. Overview of NDC AirShopping

The IATA NDC standard transforms airline retailing from static schedule filings (ATPCO/GDS) to dynamic, direct offer creation controlled by the carrier's Order Management System (OMS).

2. The Shopping Workflow

  1. AirShoppingRQ: The travel seller or aggregator sends an AirShopping request specifying travel criteria:
    • Origin and destination airport codes (IATA 3-letter).
    • Travel dates and preferred cabin classes.
    • Passenger types (Adult, Child, Infant) and loyalty credentials.
  2. Carrier Processing: The airline pricing engine computes real-time personalized offers, bundles (baggage, Wi-Fi, lounge), and dynamic ancillary pricing.
  3. AirShoppingRS: The carrier returns an AirShopping response containing:
    • OfferID: Unique temporary identifier with time-to-live expiration (typically 15-30 minutes).
    • FlightDetails: Segment timings, equipment, operating and marketing carrier codes.
    • Price: Grand total, tax breakdowns, and currency.
    • Baggage & Ancillaries: Included service allowances and optional add-ons.
  4. OfferPrice: (Optional) Re-confirms price before order creation.
  5. OrderCreate: Commits the booking, creates an Airline PNR and generates an Order ID with e-ticket issuance.

3. Coexistence with Traditional GDS EDIFACT

Unlike traditional EDIFACT messaging where GDS calculates the fare based on filed tariffs, NDC guarantees that the airline owns and constructs the offer directly.
