Emirates Airlines — Comprehensive Cancellation, Refund & Disruption Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 | Version 3.2 | Document Reference: EK-POL-2024-C1

1. General Cancellation Rules

All tickets issued by Emirates are subject to the fare conditions of the specific fare family purchased at the time of reservation.

1.1 Fare Family Cancellation Matrix

  • Special Fares: Strictly non-refundable in the event of passenger cancellation. Fuel surcharges (YQ/YR) and government taxes may be refunded upon request.
  • Saver Fares: Cancellations made more than 24 hours prior to scheduled departure incur a fixed fee of ₹5,000 (or equivalent local currency). Within 24 hours of departure, tickets are non-refundable.
  • Flex Fares: Cancellations prior to departure incur a nominal administrative penalty of ₹2,000. Balance refunded to original form of payment.
  • Flex Plus Fares: Fully refundable with zero cancellation penalty prior to departure.

2. Flight Disruptions & Carrier Cancellations

In the event that Emirates cancels a scheduled flight, changes the schedule by more than 3 hours, or fails to stop at the passenger's destination:

  • The passenger is entitled to free rebooking on the next available Emirates flight without fare difference.
  • Alternatively, the passenger may request a 100% full refund with all fees waived.
  • Hotel accommodation and meal vouchers are provided if the disruption necessitates an involuntary overnight layover.

3. 24-Hour Cooling Off Period

Bookings made directly via the Emirates website or verified NDC channels may be cancelled without penalty within 24 hours of booking, provided departure is scheduled at least 7 days from the booking date.
